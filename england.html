How this estimate works

Income Tax uses individual Personal Allowances, the £100,000 allowance taper and England’s 20% / 40% / 45% bands. Employee National Insurance uses monthly Category A thresholds. Loans and salary-sacrifice pensions are separate deductions.

Universal Credit is means-tested for the household. The estimate includes standard and child elements, eligible housing and childcare amounts, confirmed disability/carer elements, earnings taper, capital rules and the benefit cap. The two-child limit ended for assessment periods starting on or after 6 April 2026. Tax-Free Childcare is an alternative to UC: its top-up goes to a childcare account and is excluded from total cash income.
